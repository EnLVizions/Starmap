Privacy Policy

App: Pythagoras Star Map (Android, package io.github.enlvizions.starmap) and the website at https://enlvizions.github.io/Starmap/
Developer: EnLVizions
Effective date: October 23, 2026

Summary

The app does not collect, store, share or sell any personal or device data.

Details

  • No account, sign-in, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software is included.
  • The star map and its data are bundled inside the app and work offline. The app requests no Android permissions beyond what is needed to display its content.
  • Display preferences (for example text size or colour palette) may be saved on your device by the app's web view (local storage). They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • If you tap a link to an outside source (for example a NASA, ESA or journal page), it opens in your browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies.
  • The "share" feature uses your phone's share sheet only when you choose to use it; the app does not see where you send it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
