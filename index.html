Pythagoras Star Map

An interactive 3D map of all known star systems within 50 light-years. Systems are ranked as interstellar destinations by distance, planet habitability and the interstellar medium along the route. Each system has a sourced findings log (55,781 findings across 1,111 systems).

Disclaimer: this is design and reference data, not flight-certified software or navigation. Statuses on literature findings are partly keyword-derived from abstracts (only rows marked "curated" were checked against quoted text). The data may contain errors; check the cited sources.
