Pythagoras Star Map

An interactive 3D map of all known star systems within 50 light-years. Systems are ranked as interstellar destinations by distance, planet habitability and the interstellar medium along the route. Each system has a sourced findings log (55,781 findings across 1,111 systems).

Disclaimer: this is design and reference data, not flight-certified software or navigation. Statuses on literature findings are partly keyword-derived from abstracts (only rows marked "curated" were checked against quoted text). The data may contain errors; check the cited sources.

Maps

Star map v6 (latest, new interface)

Same data as v5, with an easier interface. The sky fills the screen and a dock holds everything else. Press ⌘K or / to search stars, planets, clusters, routes and commands. A ranked side list has filter chips and j/k keys. Each system has a tabbed page: Summary, Planets, Findings (with verification badges), Hazards, Images and Data sheet. Breadcrumbs, Esc to go back, deep links, an onboarding tour and a mobile layout are included. Heavy data loads after the map appears. v6.3 loads faster (data split into cached, versioned files; offline repeat visits over http) and adds the sky from Earth (horizon, Chicago by default, any date, 2032 preset, “visible tonight” list), a lighter label style choice, long-press cards on touch screens, and a JWST tour built from observation papers. Screenshots: Earth sky, halo labels. v6.2 adds guided tours (nearest neighbours, best destinations, JWST results, hazards), camera presets (top-down, night sky), label density and declutter, hover cards and 5/10/20/50 ly rings. Plain-language summary line on each star page, compare mode (pin up to 3 systems), print and share. Examples: compare · α Cen findings · Pleiades · α Cen route hazards · screenshots: star page, search, mobile.

Star map v5 (3D)

Everything in v4, plus per-star data sheets from 8 catalogues (about 32,000 sourced values), real DSS2 sky images for every system, and 695 Gaia star clusters out to 1,630 ly. 119 of them have real images, with ESO and NOIRLab photos of the Pleiades, Hyades, NGC 3532 and NGC 2547. Cluster panel · star data sheet · notes · sources.

Star map v4 (3D)

Real star colours from measured Teff, Hipparcos background sky, constellation lines. Click a star to fly into its system: real orbits, habitable zones, debris disks. System views: Alpha Cen (image), Teegarden (image). What is real vs assumed.
